How Organiser Entitlement Is Calculated

KALXA separates original sales, refunds, commission reversals and settlement reconciliation.

Net organiser entitlement Original organiser gross share − successfully refunded ticket face value + reversed KALXA commission.

Example Original ticket sale: R100.00
KALXA commission: R4.00
Original organiser share: R96.00
Successfully refunded face value: R50.00
Reversed KALXA commission: R2.00

Net organiser entitlement: R48.00 This example illustrates a partial refund. It does not represent an actual refund recorded against an organiser.

Organiser Payout Controls

Payment execution is intentionally unavailable.

🔒 Organiser payouts are disabled

KALXA will not initiate Paystack transfers, bank payments, automatic payouts or payout approvals through this ledger.

Payout functionality will remain disabled until Paystack confirms the permitted settlement arrangement and KALXA implements the necessary financial controls.

These controls must include verified settlement receipts, refund and chargeback adjustments, processing-fee responsibility, payout eligibility checks, payment audit records and duplicate-payment prevention.
