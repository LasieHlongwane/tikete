Sales Phases / Early Bird Pricing

Kalxa automatically uses the first eligible phase in order. A phase stops when its end time is reached or its quantity limit is sold. Times below are South African time.

For an Early Bird that should be selling now:

Leave Starts blank, set the Early Bird price, then set an Ends date or quantity limit. If Starts is a future date, Kalxa will correctly keep that phase unavailable until that date.
